SciQuiz is a quiz/study website to help level 2 NCEA science students prepare effectively for upcoming external exams my website has a quiz for every external in the core science subjects; Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and General Science. The goal for my website was to make sure every level 2 science student had a good place to study and quiz themselves on a trustworthy website made by a fellow level 2 science student. My information is collected from trusted sources such as the NZQA website with past exam papers, my website collects these past questions and puts them into a quiz for the students to use as practice for their exams. The reason I made this website is due to the fact that I have heard from fellow science students who struggle with studying for external exams and unsure where they can get questions to practice, my website gives this to them including information that they will be given in an exam such as an equation sheet and paper they can write on. This gives them a familiarity they can practice using before the exam.
